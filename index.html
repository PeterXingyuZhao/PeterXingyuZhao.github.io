NeurIPS 2026Accepted

Shattered Compositionality: Counterintuitive Learning Dynamics of Transformers for Arithmetic

Xingyu Zhao, Darsh Sharma, Rheeya Uppaal, and Yiqiao Zhong

Transformers can acquire arithmetic subskills in reverse or in parallel relative to standard procedures. We study how training-data correlations shape these learning patterns and how competition between subskills can lead to brittle generalization.

Paper ↗Code ↗Advisor’s blog ↗
My research contributions

I tracked subskill acquisition across addition, multiplication, comparison, and sorting; developed conditional mutual information diagnostics; and designed targeted ablations. My experiments included roughly 10M–100M parameter transformers, Pythia-1B fine-tuning, and reasoning robustness evaluations across 10 pretrained LLMs.

PreprintUnder review at ICLR 2027

Cross-Trait Transfer in Subliminal Learning

Xingyu Zhao and Yiqiao Zhong

A teacher model’s preferences can shape a student’s other traits, even through semantically unrelated training outputs. We investigate the structure of this transfer using behavioral measurements, output distributions, and internal representations.

Paper ↗Code ↗
My research contributions

I developed metrics of output-probability shifts and representation geometry, fine-tuned 7–8B models using LoRA, and designed multi-trait and rare-trait experiments. I studied how related traits can be amplified beyond the teacher’s own trait and how transfer patterns become more differentiated during training.
